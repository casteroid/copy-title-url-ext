Try it here

  1. Select a sentence in the window below.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+C (Option+Shift+C on macOS), or right-click the selection and pick Copy Selection and URL. With nothing selected, the shortcut copies the page title instead.

What it does

        

          

            
Right-click menu

            
Copy Title and URL anywhere on a page. Copy Selection and URL on selected text, including text inside frames.

          

          

            
Button and shortcut

            
The toolbar button or Alt+Shift+C copies the selection, or the title when nothing is selected. Change the shortcut in your browser's extension shortcut settings.

          

          

            
Two formats

            
Plain text is the title or selection on one line and the URL on the next. Slack, Google Docs, Notion and Gmail paste a linked title instead, and Obsidian pastes [title](url).

          

          

            
Clean URLs

            
utm_*, fbclid, gclid and mc_eid are removed. Every other parameter and the #fragment stay as they were.

          

          

            
Feedback

            
A pinned toolbar icon flashes ✓ after a copy, or ! on pages the browser keeps closed, such as chrome:// and about:.

          

          

            
Languages

            
English, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), Spanish, French and German.

          

        

      


      

        
Privacy

        
No settings, no storage, no network access and no content script. The extension only runs in a tab after you click one of its menu items or its toolbar button, or press its shortcut. The title, URL and selection go to your clipboard and nowhere else.
